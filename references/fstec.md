# Example domain: personal-data protection (FSTEC methodology)

Optional reference — use it only when the topic is personal-data protection (ИБ/ПДн).
For other domains (economics, engineering, education) it does not apply.

Computation order:

1. **Threat assessment** by the FSTEC Russia methodology of 05.02.2021: consequences,
   objects, attackers, and actual scenarios.
2. **Security level** per the Government decree No. 1119.
3. **Baseline measure set** and its adaptation to the system under study per FSTEC
   order No. 21 (rev. 14.05.2020), clause 9.

Finish the full chain (level → model → measure set) in the text; do not leave it to
an appendix "to be collected on site".
